How it works

From download to your first colored square

  1. Download

    Buy on Etsy, then download your PDFs from your Etsy purchases. On a phone, use etsy.com in your web browser; the Etsy app may not open downloads.

  2. Print

    Print at home in US Letter or A4, then fill in by hand (you can't type into the PDF). Black-and-white printing works too.

  3. Color as you go

    Each time you pay or save, color your progress. Missed a week? Pick up where you left off.
