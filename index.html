How it works

From download to your first colored square

  1. Download

    Buy on Etsy, then download your PDFs from your Etsy purchases. On a phone, use etsy.com in your web browser; the Etsy app may not open downloads.

  2. Print, or open it

    Print at home in US Letter or A4 and fill in by hand (you can't type into the PDF). Digital Paper: open the link in your PDF and add it to your Home Screen.

  3. Color as you go

    Each time you pay or save, color your progress. Missed a week? Pick up where you left off.

Digital Paper

Tap to write, tap to color

Digital Paper opens like a sheet of paper on your phone or computer, with no app to install and no account.

  • Tap a line to write in it
  • Tap a bubble, a coin or a thermometer to color it in
  • Totals add up for you, and it saves as you go on the device you use it on
  • Print it whenever you like
