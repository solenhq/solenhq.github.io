Privacy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This website

This site is a set of plain pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no sign-up, no cookies of its own, no analytics and no advertising. Like any web host, GitHub may keep basic technical logs (such as IP addresses) to run and protect its service; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Buying on Etsy

All purchases happen on Etsy. Your order, payment and download details are handled by Etsy under Etsy's privacy policy. As the seller, SOLENHQ sees only the order details Etsy shares with sellers and uses them only to deliver and support your order.

Digital Paper

Digital Paper (coming soon) pages save what you write and color only in your own web browser, on your own device (browser storage). Nothing you write is sent to SOLENHQ or anyone else. Clearing your browser's data for this site, using a private window, or, in Safari, not opening the page for about a week of browsing can remove what you wrote.

Fonts

Pages load their fonts from Google Fonts, which receives a standard web request (including your IP address) when a page opens.

Contact

Questions? Send a message through the SOLENHQ Etsy shop.
